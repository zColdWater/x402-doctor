x402 error invalid_payload

Payment payload is malformed.

What the spec says

Payment payload is malformed or contains invalid data.

Common causes

  • The header is not base64 of a JSON object, or it was double-encoded.
  • A v2 payload without the required accepted field (the requirement being paid).
  • Missing or mistyped fields in payload.authorization (numbers instead of strings, nonce not 32 bytes).

How to fix it

Paste the header into the Doctor to see the decoded JSON and every missing or mistyped field.

Check your own payment

Paste the 402 challenge, the PAYMENT-SIGNATURE / X-PAYMENT header and the response into x402 Doctor. It decodes them in your browser, checks every field, and for signature errors recovers the signer and names the EIP-712 domain the signature was really made with. Nothing is uploaded.
