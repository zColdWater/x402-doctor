x402 Doctor

Error codes Support Source

Paste what your x402 server, client or facilitator sent. The Doctor decodes the headers, checks every field against the x402 v1/v2 rules, and recovers the EIP-3009 signer from the signature. When the signer is not authorization.from, it tries the usual EIP-712 domain mistakes and tells you which one was made.

  • Nothing you paste leaves your browser
  • x402 v1 and v2
  • EIP-3009 USDC on EVM chains
  • Free, open source (MIT)

Signatures and authorizations are sensitive: a valid, unexpired payment header can be replayed by whoever holds it. That is why this page never sends anything anywhere. You can save it and use it offline.

Limits. The Doctor checks EIP-3009 "exact" payments on EVM chains. It cannot see on-chain state (balances, used nonces) and does not check Solana or Permit2 payloads. Smart-wallet (ERC-1271 / ERC-6492) signatures are detected but cannot be verified offline.
